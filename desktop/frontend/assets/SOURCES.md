# Artwork sources

The iOxide logo is copied unchanged from `docs/assets/iOxide-logo.png`.
`ioxide-icon.png` is the icon-only derivative requested for the native taskbar,
application bundle and tray. The full wordmark remains in the application UI.

Calendar client icons identify the applications supported by the setup guide.
They are third-party artwork, not part of iOxide's MIT-licensed original artwork:

- Apple Calendar: Apple App Store artwork for app ID `1108185179`, retrieved
  through `https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1108185179` on 2026-11-12.
  Product page: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/calendar/id1108185179.
  Apple Calendar and its icon belong to Apple Inc.
- Thunderbird: https://www.thunderbird.net/media/img/thunderbird/favicon-196.png,
  retrieved on 2026-11-12. Thunderbird and its logo belong to their respective
  rights holders. Product page: https://www.thunderbird.net/.
- DAVx⁵: https://www.davx5.com/fileadmin/user_upload/logos/davx5_icon_green_bg.svg,
  retrieved unchanged on 2026-11-12. Used to identify DAVx⁵ with context and a link
  to its website, as described at https://www.davx5.com/about/logos.
  DAVx⁵ is a trademark of bitfire web engineering GmbH.
- `caldav.svg`: original generic calendar icon for iOxide, MIT licensed.
